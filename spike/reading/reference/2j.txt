## Sheet 1, front
My own darling Mother
[struck: illegible] I am so sorry
I have not written, but I thought
as Gladys said in her letter on
Saturday, that, "we are going up
to London [struck: on Saturday] ^tomorrow^". I was waiting
to know where you were stopping in
London. We all went up the same
way to see the Procession, as when
the German Emperor came. We had
to wait for about an hour & a half
before it came. But it was very fine
when it came. The only people I
recognised I am sorry to say, I [struck: illegible]
were [struck: Lord] Roberts & the German Emperor.
I ought to have seen the King but he
was on the opposite side to me I think.
The coffin was on a Kaki gun carriage
drawn by blue jackets – I don't suppose Baby
& Betty will be able to come down – It snowed
hard last night & now is thawing so it
is very nasty – goodbye from your loving son
Jim Jack
