**Tools → Clean up**: the batches newest first, each row with **Delete below**, which deletes every closed or purged batch below that row as Delete does each, leaving open ones, after a confirm page that says what goes, what stays and which orphans go with them. Orphans, what no batch claims in staging or the Queue after a deletion failed part way, are listed there and removable on their own. Deleting a batch now removes its runs from the Queue instead of cancelling them (#100); its id is still not given out again, a record of the deletion staying in staging.
