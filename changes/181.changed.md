A blank side is left out of a letter (story 181): at *arrange* each side has a *blank: leave out* mark, off unless the operator sets it. A side marked has no master and no renditions and `letter.yaml` records it as `blank: true`; a sheet blank on both sides is left out whole and the sheets after it close the gap; a letter whose every side is marked is refused.
