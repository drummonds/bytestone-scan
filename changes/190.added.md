A piece of writing received as a Word document or plain text is imported (story 190): *Import a writing* on the batch page takes one `.docx` or `.txt` as a writing import among the batch's scans. Its words are converted to djot by allowlist (headings, paragraphs, bold and italic, list items, line breaks, links to the web, footnotes and endnotes; tracked changes read as accepted), pictures, tables and embedded or linked objects counted and left out, a document with macros refused, and the Word document never opened by an office program. The operator reads the page as the site will make it, then says who it names, when it was written and the hands it passed through. The writing is an item of kind `writing`, `writing.dj` and `writing.yaml` alone with no renditions; the received file is deleted once the bag is written, its name, size and sha256 kept in the record. Needs life-weaver v0.61.0 or later, where an account became a writing
