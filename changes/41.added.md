Deposit and receipt (story #41 of the batch-lifecycle epic, life-weaver#33): photo ids come from the archive service (`POST /people/{home}/items`), not a local series; once bagged each photo is deposited (`PUT /items/{id}`, a tar of the bag) and its receipt read back and checked against the bag, checksum for checksum; a bag the archive refuses as a duplicate ends the photo, rejected, shown on its page and the load’s; a service that is down fails the step until retried. Staging keeps bags under `bags/` with the archive’s answer beside each. The service client is `internal/archive`, the bag writer `internal/bag`; tests use `archivetest`, an in-memory service that follows life-weaver’s deposit table
