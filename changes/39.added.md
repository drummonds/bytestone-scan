Backs (story B of the batch-lifecycle epic, roadmap 5): after the fronts the load waits for the prints to be turned over in place, previews the bed to see where each back lies, flags a back whose size differs from its front’s or that cannot be found, and scans the fronts’ regions again at 300 ppi, 8 bits, as `back.tif` in the bag. Each photo is now a workflow of its own under its load (`scan_photo`: develop front → awaiting back → develop back → accepted → package); develop and package leave the load. The load page shows the backs preview and asks for the prints to be turned, or for the backs to be skipped when they are blank (then the bag has no `back.tif`); the photo page shows the back; the backs can be rescanned. Masters at 8 bits per channel; fakes and the recorded scanner serve the side turned to the glass
