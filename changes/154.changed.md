The turn is said at confirm, where the print is first seen, not only at the quality check: Turn ↺ and Turn ↻ on each print's close-up, kept on the found print in prints.yaml (`turn`, degrees clockwise) through a move or a split, said in the heading and the prints table, and carried onto the front at capture so the first renditions are the right way up. The close-up is shown turned, as the rendition will be, its rulers and arrows laid round it as shown: the arrows at the top move the edge seen at the top, and the move posted still names the print's own edge; the back's close-up and the quality page's margin arrows follow the turn. A quality-check turn of the front is written back to the found print, so a reshape keeps it (#154)
